FAQ

Answers to common questions.

Hosting FAQ pages commonly cover upgrades, billing, backups, support and refund rules, because these are some of the first things customers want clarified before purchasing or renewing.

What services does ReignDC provide?

We currently offer game hosting, web hosting, VPS, VDS and related infrastructure services.

Which locations are available?

Mumbai and Singapore are currently available hosting locations for supported services.

How do I order a service?

You can order through our panel, client area or by contacting us on WhatsApp or Discord depending on the service flow.

Do you offer refunds on VPS or RDP hosting?

No. VPS and RDP hosting are non-refundable once delivered, as stated in our refund policy.

Do you offer refunds on game server hosting?

A full refund may be possible only within the first 24 hours, and only if the issue is from our side and cannot be fixed. Customer-side configuration, script and plugin issues are not eligible.

What happens if my server has downtime?

We do not offer a refund for downtime. If downtime is caused by our service, your plan may be extended by the verified number of affected hours.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Most hosting providers commonly support service upgrades through support or billing systems, and ReignDC may allow upgrades depending on the service type and available resources.

Do you keep backups?

Many hosting services perform regular backups, but customers should still maintain their own copies as a best practice because backups may not always be guaranteed for every incident.

How do I request a refund?

You must open a ticket within the eligible period and provide the required details for verification.

Can services be suspended?

Yes. Services may be suspended for abuse, non-payment, policy violations, security risk or prohibited activities, which is a standard clause in hosting and service agreements.
