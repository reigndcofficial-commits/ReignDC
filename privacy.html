Privacy Policy

How ReignDC collects, uses and protects information.

Privacy policies typically explain what data is collected, why it is used, how long it is retained, whether cookies are used and how users can request changes or deletion.[web:136][web:139][web:142][web:145]

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how ReignDC collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use our website, client systems, support channels and hosting services.

2. Information we collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Name, email address, billing details and contact details provided during signup or payment.
  • Service information such as orders, invoices, subscriptions, renewal history and support requests.
  • Technical information such as IP addresses, browser type, device information, login activity and security-related logs.
  • Messages and files submitted through tickets, forms, Discord, WhatsApp or other support channels.

3. How we use your information

  • To create, provision, manage and maintain your hosting services.
  • To process payments, invoices, renewals and account verification.
  • To provide customer support, troubleshooting and service updates.
  • To improve security, prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorized access.
  • To send important service notices, billing reminders and policy updates.

4. Cookies and analytics

Our website or connected services may use cookies, session technologies or similar tools to improve functionality, remember preferences, analyze traffic and support security controls. Privacy guidance commonly recommends disclosing cookie use and its purpose within the policy.

5. Payment and billing data

Payment processing may be handled by us or by third-party billing and payment providers. We do not promise that full payment card data is stored directly on our website, and payment handling may be subject to the relevant provider’s own privacy and security practices.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited data only when necessary:

  • With payment processors and billing providers.
  • With infrastructure, security or fraud-prevention providers.
  • When required by law, regulation, legal process or valid government request.
  • To enforce our policies, protect our rights or prevent abuse.

7. Data retention

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for business operations, legal compliance, billing records, abuse prevention, dispute resolution and service administration.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and operational measures to protect account and service data. However, no online system can be guaranteed as completely secure.

9. User rights

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of certain personal information, subject to legal, billing, fraud-prevention or operational retention requirements.

10. Third-party services

Our website and services may link to or rely on third-party platforms such as payment gateways, support platforms, communication tools and panels. Their data practices may be governed by their own policies.

11. Policy changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of our website or services after updates means you accept the revised policy.

12. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact ReignDC through our official support or contact channels listed on the Contact page.
